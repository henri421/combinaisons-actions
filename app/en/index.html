Combinations of actions

Combinations of actions of EN 1990 for buildings: ULS EQU, STR/GEO, GEO and accidental, SLS characteristic, frequent and quasi-permanent. CSV and Excel export.

The γ and ψ factors are the recommended values of EN 1990:2002; the National Annex may modify them. The active profile must match the text that applies to the project.

Actions

One row per characteristic action. The group makes actions mutually exclusive: two actions of the same group are never combined together (wind cases with each other, for example).

Effects (optional)

With effects, each action gets one value per effect; the tool returns the combined values and their envelope. Linear superposition: linear analysis only.

Options
Import from charges-climatiques

The “Actions (JSON)” file of the charges-climatiques tool: each snow and wind case becomes a variable action, in a snow group and a wind group.

The CSV and the workbook have one row per combination and one column per action (its factor), then the combined effects. The workbook has one sheet per family, one sheet of actions and one of envelopes.
